# Medical Disclaimer Draft

Founder review required before use.

Swasthi is not a doctor, pharmacist, hospital, emergency service, or medical device.

Swasthi does not:

- Diagnose conditions.
- Prescribe medicines.
- Recommend treatment.
- Change prescribed doses or timings.
- Replace doctors, pharmacists, or qualified healthcare professionals.
- Provide emergency care.

OCR and AI-assisted parsing can be wrong. Medicine names, doses, timing, frequency, food instructions, and refill estimates must be checked against the original prescription.

Users and caregivers must consult qualified healthcare professionals before making medical decisions, changing treatment, stopping medicines, substituting medicines, or acting on unclear prescription information.

For emergencies or urgent symptoms, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare provider immediately. Do not rely on Swasthi for emergency help.
