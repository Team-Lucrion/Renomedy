# Privacy Policy Draft

Founder review required before use. This draft is for the Swasthi/Renomedy closed beta in India and is not legal advice.

## What We Collect

During beta, we may collect:

- Sign-in details such as name, email, phone number, and Clerk user ID.
- Family care profile details entered by users or caregivers.
- Prescription photos uploaded by users.
- OCR text, parsed medicine names, doses, timings, and verification status.
- Medication schedules, dose logs, refill status, and notification tokens.
- Beta invite status, operational logs, payment status if enabled, and support messages.

## Why We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide beta access and account security.
- Help users organize prescription information and medicine reminders.
- Let invited caregivers view shared family care information.
- Run OCR and medicine parsing workflows.
- Send reminders and operational alerts.
- Debug safety, privacy, and reliability issues during beta.

## Medical Safety

Swasthi does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, replace doctors, or provide emergency care. Users and caregivers must verify all medicines, doses, timings, and instructions against the original prescription and consult qualified healthcare professionals.

## Sharing And Processors

We may use service providers for sign-in, database/storage, OCR, notifications, analytics, error monitoring, and payments. These providers may process data needed to operate the beta. We do not claim HIPAA compliance, medical-device approval, clinical validation, or verified India-only data residency.

## Prescription Privacy

Prescription photos and extracted text may contain sensitive health information. Access should be limited to the signed-in user and family members they invite. Users should invite only trusted caregivers.

## Data Location

Production data region and residency must be confirmed before broader launch. Do not represent India-only storage unless verified in production vendor settings.

## Retention And Deletion

Users may request deletion by emailing support@renomedy.com. We aim to acknowledge within 7 calendar days and complete eligible deletion within 30 calendar days after identity verification. Some audit, payment, security, legal, or backup records may be retained for a limited period.

## Children And Dependents

Caregivers may enter information about family members or dependents only when they have appropriate authority and consent to do so.

## Contact

Privacy and deletion requests: support@renomedy.com
