# Closed Beta Terms Draft

Founder review required before use. This draft is for supervised family beta testing in India.

## Beta Purpose

The closed beta is intended to test whether Swasthi helps families organize prescriptions, medicine reminders, refill awareness, and caregiver coordination.

## Who Should Use The Beta

Only invited users and trusted caregivers should use the beta. Users should not share beta or family invite codes publicly.

## Required Supervision

Every medicine entry must be checked against the original prescription by the user or caregiver. For any uncertainty, users must consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

## High-Risk Medicines

Some medicines may be blocked or limited during beta, including medicines that require careful manual management. Swasthi may refuse automation for safety reasons.

## No Medical Reliance

Swasthi does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, replace doctors, or provide emergency care. The beta is not clinically validated and is not approved as a medical device.

## Data Handling

Beta use may involve sensitive health information, including prescription photos and medicine schedules. Users should invite only people they trust. Data deletion requests can be sent to support@renomedy.com.

## Feedback And Changes

The beta may change frequently. Features may be removed, access may be revoked, and data may be corrected or deleted as part of safety operations.
