# Terms Of Service Draft

Founder review required before use. This draft is for the Swasthi/Renomedy closed beta in India and is not legal advice.

## Closed Beta Use

Swasthi is provided for limited supervised family beta testing. Access may be changed, suspended, or ended at any time during beta.

## Not Medical Advice

Swasthi does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend treatment, replace doctors, or provide emergency care. It is an organization and reminder tool. Users and caregivers must verify all information against the original prescription and consult qualified doctors, pharmacists, or other licensed healthcare professionals.

## User Responsibilities

Users agree to:

- Enter accurate information.
- Upload only prescriptions they are allowed to share.
- Invite only trusted caregivers or family members.
- Verify OCR and medicine details before relying on reminders.
- Not use Swasthi for emergencies, urgent medical decisions, or treatment changes.

## Beta Limitations

Beta software may be incomplete, inaccurate, interrupted, or changed. OCR and medicine parsing can fail. Reminder delivery can be delayed or missed. Users remain responsible for medication decisions and adherence.

## Prohibited Use

Users must not:

- Upload data they do not have permission to use.
- Attempt to access another user's data.
- Share invite codes publicly.
- Reverse engineer, abuse, overload, or attack the service.
- Use the service to provide diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.

## Account And Access

Users are responsible for keeping their sign-in methods secure. Invite access can be revoked if misused or if beta capacity changes.

## Data Deletion

Deletion requests can be sent to support@renomedy.com. Eligible deletion is handled according to the beta deletion process.

## No Regulated Claims

Swasthi makes no HIPAA compliance claim, medical-device approval claim, clinical validation claim, or India data residency claim unless separately verified and documented.
