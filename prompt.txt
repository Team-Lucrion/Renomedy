The Android UI currently looks inconsistent and less polished compared to iPhone. I want a focused Android UI stabilization and design-system cleanup pass across the app WITHOUT rewriting the architecture.

Goal:
Make the app feel clean, calm, modern, and trustworthy on Android devices (especially mid-range Android phones common in India).

Main priorities:
- consistency
- spacing
- typography
- safe areas
- card styling
- readability
- form behavior
- keyboard handling
- Android visual polish

DO NOT redesign the entire app.
DO NOT add flashy effects.
DO NOT change business logic.
DO NOT rewrite navigation/auth/backend flows.

This is a UI consistency + Android optimization task only.
