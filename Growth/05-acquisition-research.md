# Renomedy Acquisition Research Notes — 13 October 2026

## Purpose and confidence

This is an initial desk-research snapshot to guide experiments, not proof of product demand. Competitor pages describe their own positioning and should be treated as marketing claims. Public demographic statistics show channel reach, not which channel converts caregivers for Renomedy.

## Findings

### 1. The broader caregiving problem is credible, but Renomedy's exact wedge remains unvalidated

Research published in *BMC Geriatrics* in September 2025 examines how adult sons' migration affects older parents and caregiving patterns in India. A 2026 open-access paper in *Archives of Gerontology and Geriatrics Plus* examines family care provision and how living arrangements and proximity shape support. These support treating family coordination as a relevant problem area, but they do not validate Renomedy or establish willingness to pay.  
Sources: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12877-025-06220-0 ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2950307825001183

### 2. The competitive landscape is not empty

- **PeakAge** markets paid elder-care services, family coordination, an app, and WhatsApp support. Its public site lists service plans and a family-coordinator add-on. This is a service-led competitor/alternative, not necessarily a direct software substitute. Source: https://peakage.in/
- **Maitri** positions itself around family logistics and medicine-related requests through WhatsApp, emphasizing low-friction interaction. Source: https://www.mymaitri.co.in/
- **FamilyCare** positions itself as a family health companion and shared health context. Its public claims are company-authored and are not independent evidence of market size or product outcomes. Source: https://www.familycare.co.in/blog/introducing-familycare
- Families may also use WhatsApp, paper lists, phone calls, and existing hospital/pharmacy workflows. These are important substitutes and may be harder to displace than another app.

**Implication:** do not market Renomedy as a generic “all-in-one elder care app.” Test a narrow, observable job where Renomedy is meaningfully easier than the family's existing workaround. Medicine information organization and family handoffs are candidate jobs, not a final positioning decision.

### 3. Digital reach is large, but reach is not acquisition proof

DataReportal's Digital 2026 India report estimates 500 million social-media user identities in October 2025 and reports large YouTube advertising reach. These figures show that digital channels can reach many people, but do not tell us whether adult children with caregiving responsibilities will discover, trust, or use Renomedy.  
Source: https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-india

**Implication:** choose one channel based on actual access to the target audience and observed qualified conversations, not the largest platform headline. Repurpose useful content; avoid building a multi-platform publishing machine before there is evidence of demand.

### 4. Trust and privacy are central product and marketing constraints

India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology lists the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 and an implementation timeline. The rules include a phased commencement schedule; the team should not assume every provision has the same effective date. The exact obligations for Renomedy depend on its role, data flows, users, and deployment.  
Source: https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025686cadad39.pdf

The National Medical Commission site lists new guidelines on ethical advertising and public communication by hospitals, medical institutions, and registered practitioners dated 6 October 2026. Any clinician/clinic collaboration needs careful review; do not imply a doctor endorses Renomedy without permission or create clinical-outcome claims.  
Source: https://nmc.org.in/whats-new?type=news

**Implication:** early demos should use fictional data; marketing forms should not collect health information; do not send medication names, prescription contents, diagnoses, or identifiable patient information to general analytics. Verify security/privacy claims before publishing them.

## Strategic conclusion

**Recommended first acquisition system:** permission-based discovery conversations + one useful reusable caregiver resource + a very small number of opt-in demos. This is chosen for learning speed and low cost, not because it has been proven to scale.

Do not begin with paid ads, influencer marketing, mass WhatsApp outreach, or large-scale SEO. Current traffic is too low to diagnose conversion, and the exact audience/problem/message still need validation.

## Questions desk research cannot answer

1. Which specific caregiver segment has the most urgent recurring coordination job?
2. Does Renomedy solve that job better than WhatsApp, notes, or a shared spreadsheet?
3. Can a new user safely reach value without founder assistance?
4. Does the value recur often enough to support retention and payment?
5. Which source can produce qualified trials within the founders' limited time?

These require direct user research and observed product use, not more desk research alone.
