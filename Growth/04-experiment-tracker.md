## Funnel definitions

- **Qualified prospect:** adult child who currently helps a parent manage medicines or healthcare tasks.
- **Qualified conversation:** the prospect describes a real workflow or problem; not just a like or generic compliment.
- **Trial started:** prospect intentionally agrees to try the app and obtains access.
- **Activated:** completes the pre-defined first-value action.
- **Returned:** comes back for a second meaningful session or task without a founder prompting every step.
- **Payment signal:** accepts a specific paid-pilot offer or takes a concrete next step; “sounds good” is not enough.

## Weekly review questions

1. What did we do, and how much time did it take?
2. How many relevant people did we reach?
3. How many had a real problem matching the target segment?
4. What stopped people from trying the app?
5. Where did people get stuck?
6. Did anyone return without being prompted?
7. What evidence contradicts our current hypothesis?
8. What single change will we make next week?

## First experiment: permission-based caregiver conversations

- **Window:** 14 days from launch of this plan
- **Action:** invite 10 relevant people to a short research conversation, through appropriate personal/community connections
- **Learning target:** complete 5 conversations if recruitment permits
- **Primary metric:** qualified conversations completed
- **Secondary metrics:** demo requests, trial starts, first-value completion
- **Time cap:** 90 minutes of outreach/follow-up per week
- **Success definition:** learn which problem and wording resonate, and recruit a few qualified trial users; do not define success by raw reach alone
- **Stop/revise:** if invitations get no response, change the source or ask a trusted adult/community organizer for an appropriate introduction; don't increase message volume indiscriminately
