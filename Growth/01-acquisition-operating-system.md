# 01 — Acquisition Operating System

## Goal

Create a repeatable, low-maintenance path from relevant caregiver awareness to a qualified Renomedy trial. Do not optimize for vanity metrics such as raw impressions or follower counts.

## Funnel definition

1. **Reach:** a relevant adult child sees a post, recommendation, community resource, or search result.
2. **Qualified visit/conversation:** they recognize that they help manage a parent's medicines or care.
3. **Intent:** they request a demo, ask a question, or agree to try the app.
4. **Activation:** they complete one meaningful task in Renomedy.
5. **Return:** they use it again when another real task occurs.
6. **Referral/payment signal:** they voluntarily recommend it or take a concrete step toward a paid pilot.

Define “first value” before instrumenting it. Suggested candidate: user creates or reviews a medicine record using clinician-provided information and successfully saves/organizes it after checking it. If interviews show a more valuable task, update the definition.

## Priority channel sequence

### Channel 1 — Founder-led, permission-based outreach (first two weeks)

Why first: with very low traffic, direct conversations are the fastest way to learn whether the problem and promise resonate. This is a research channel, not a permanent manual-sales model.

Weekly limit: contact 3–5 relevant people through genuine personal or community connections; ask permission for a 15-minute conversation. Do not bulk-message strangers.

Message:
> Hi — I'm helping build Renomedy, a tool intended to make it easier for families to organize a parent's medicines and stay coordinated. I'm trying to understand how families handle this today. Would you be open to a 15-minute conversation? This isn't a sales pitch, and you don't need to share private medical details.

After the conversation, invite a demo only if the person has a relevant need. Track objections in anonymized notes.

### Channel 2 — Reusable educational content (start week 2)

Create one useful, non-alarmist resource per week. Repurpose it into:
- one simple visual/checklist
- one short post for an appropriate community or social channel
- one landing-page FAQ or article section

Content should solve a real caregiver organization problem first; mention Renomedy transparently as a tool being developed. No fear-based messaging, medical advice, or unsupported outcome claims.

### Channel 3 — Small, relevant partner conversations (week 3 onward)

Speak with 1–2 local elder-care coordinators, caregiver group organizers, or healthcare professionals each week only if they can be approached respectfully and with adult support as needed. Ask about workflow, not for an endorsement. Offer a neutral demo if they express interest. Any promotion or data sharing requires clear permission and appropriate safeguards.

### Channel 4 — Search/SEO (background, low effort)

Use the landing page to answer specific, non-clinical caregiver workflow questions, such as organizing medicine lists for a family appointment or sharing care responsibilities. Do not create many thin AI-generated articles. Publish one genuinely useful page and measure qualified visits.

### Not yet

- Paid advertising
- Broad influencer campaigns
- Mass cold email/WhatsApp
- Buying lists or scraping personal contacts
- Building a large content calendar
- Chasing app-store installs without an activation path

## Landing page requirements

1. First screen names the audience and task clearly.
2. Explain the product in plain language without medical promises.
3. Show real product screenshots or clearly labeled mockups.
4. State what is available today versus planned.
5. Provide one primary CTA: request a guided trial or join a small pilot.
6. Explain how health information is handled in understandable terms; only make privacy/security claims that are verified.
7. Include a contact route and a short FAQ addressing who it is for, how it works, and what it does not do.

## Weekly operating routine

**Monday (10 min):** choose one hypothesis and one channel.  
**Midweek (60–90 min):** outreach/interviews and one content asset.  
**Weekend (30 min):** review funnel, user feedback, and next decision.

Only run one main experiment at a time. Record the audience, message, channel, CTA, effort, and result.

## Decision rules

- No qualified conversations after 10 respectful, relevant invitations: revisit audience/recruitment source/message.
- Conversations happen but no one wants a demo: revisit problem urgency and positioning.
- Demos happen but no one reaches first value: fix onboarding or product flow.
- First value happens but no one returns: investigate use frequency and recurring value.
- Users return and ask to invite others: test a repeatable referral path.
- Do not interpret a handful of positive comments as product-market fit.
