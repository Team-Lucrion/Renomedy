## Decision

The old acquisition playbooks are superseded as the default operating plan. Keep them in repository history for reference; do not delete evidence or historical notes. Use this system until the founders review results.

## Current baseline (13 October 2026)

- Estimated landing-page traffic: 0–50 visits/month
- App signups: 0
- Repeat users: 0
- Team capacity: two founders, under five combined hours/week
- Product: working app; current wedge includes prescription/medicine organization and family management

These numbers show that acquisition volume is low. They do **not** yet identify whether the biggest conversion issue is positioning, trust, access, activation, or product value.

## Strategy

**One audience hypothesis:** adult children who actively help a parent manage medicines and coordinate care.

**One testable promise:** “Organize a parent's medicines and help family members stay coordinated.”

**Primary conversion:** a qualified caregiver requests or completes a guided trial and reaches one meaningful product action.

**Not assumed:** product-market fit, a winning channel, recurring willingness to pay, or demand for broad care coordination.

## Non-negotiable rules

- No paid ads until we can measure the funnel and observe qualified users reaching first value.
- No mass unsolicited messaging, scraped contact lists, fake testimonials, fabricated clinical endorsements, or spam.
- Do not make claims that Renomedy prevents medication errors, improves health outcomes, or replaces professional advice without adequate evidence and review.
- Do not collect real prescription or patient details in marketing forms. Use fictional/demo data during demonstrations unless the product has appropriate safeguards and the user has consented.
- Do not send health information, medication names, prescription text, diagnoses, or identifiable patient data to general analytics.
- Any clinician or pharmacy collaboration must be transparent; never imply endorsement without explicit permission.
- Every campaign gets a source tag and a defined learning question.
- Stop activities that consume time without producing qualified conversations or trials.

## Capacity plan

Target **2.5–4 hours combined per week**:
- 75 minutes: outreach and follow-up
- 45 minutes: one reusable educational asset
- 45 minutes: demo/onboarding or user observation
- 30 minutes: measurement and decision
- Up to 45 minutes: one high-impact fix

## Four-week milestone

By 12 November 2026, seek:
- 10–15 customer conversations, if recruitment allows
- 5 qualified product trials as a learning target
- At least 3 observed onboarding sessions
- A basic funnel with verified analytics
- One evidence-based decision about positioning/channel/activation

These are learning targets, not benchmarks or guarantees. If recruitment is slower, report that honestly and extend the experiment instead of manufacturing activity.
