# Renomedy First 50 Users Playbook

## Objective

Practical GTM plan to get the first 50 real users into Renomedy with trust preserved.

---

## 1. Ideal First User Personas

### Persona A: Adult child caregiver

- manages medicines for parents
- often receives prescriptions over WhatsApp
- struggles with clarity and follow-up

### Persona B: Spouse medication manager

- coordinates daily routines
- cares about reminders and refill continuity

### Persona C: Elder chronic-medication household

- multiple prescriptions
- repeated medicine changes
- high confusion and continuity burden

### Persona D: Trust-forward referred user

- enters via doctor or pharmacist suggestion
- likely to try quickly if framed as clarity, not "AI magic"

---

## 2. Best Early Targeting

Prioritize:

- caregiver-led households
- elderly medication-heavy homes
- repeated prescription users
- households where one person already manages everyone else’s medicines

Do not prioritize first:

- casual wellness users
- low-medication households
- users who only want novelty

---

## 3. WhatsApp Acquisition

WhatsApp should be treated as the first serious growth surface.

Tactics:

- direct founder outreach to qualified leads
- request referrals from activated users
- use prescription clarity as the hook
- send beta code plus simple next-step instructions

Best message angle:

"If prescriptions are hard to read or hard to coordinate across family, Renomedy can help make them clearer and easier to manage."

---

## 4. Doctor / Pharmacist Referral Pilots

Pilot with:

- one friendly doctor
- one pharmacist
- one clinic desk or assistant

Ask them for:

- 5 to 10 families who repeatedly ask medicine clarification questions

Track:

- number referred
- number approved
- number redeemed
- first upload completion

---

## 5. Reno It Badge Loop

Reno It is the share-led loop.

Loop:

1. user decodes prescription
2. user shares family-friendly card
3. family sees `Decoded with Renomedy`
4. family clicks or remembers CTA
5. new lead enters beta funnel

Operating rule:

- Reno It should be used as a trust-forward family sharing loop, not spam

---

## 6. Landing Page Funnel

Current beta site:

- `https://getrenomedy.netlify.app`

Goal of landing page:

- collect serious caregivers
- frame Renomedy as prescription clarity + family medicine coordination
- drive Tally or direct WhatsApp intake

Landing page should answer:

- what problem this solves
- why it is invite-only
- what the user gets first
- why trust matters

---

## 7. Founder-Led Onboarding

For first 50 users, assume founder-assisted onboarding is a strength, not a weakness.

Founder should:

- send beta code
- confirm first sign in
- ask user to upload first real prescription
- watch for friction
- capture direct trust reactions

This is PMF discovery, not just customer support.

---

## 8. Community Outreach

Good early surfaces:

- caregiver WhatsApp groups
- elder care groups
- chronic illness communities
- family support communities

Approach:

- education first
- prescription clarity story first
- invitation only after value is understood

---

## 9. Trust-First Messaging

Always lead with:

- clear prescriptions
- family coordination
- reminders and continuity

Always support with:

- doctor-first disclaimer
- verification language
- privacy / private Sanctuary framing

Avoid:

- flashy AI positioning
- "magic" claims
- broad health platform language too early

---

## 10. Daily Acquisition Actions

Every day:

1. message 5 to 10 qualified leads
2. follow up with inactive approved users
3. ask 1 active user for 1 referral
4. collect 1 trust quote or objection
5. review first-upload conversion

---

## 11. Weekly Review System

Review weekly:

- leads collected
- beta codes sent
- beta codes redeemed
- sanctuaries created
- first uploads completed
- first Reno It shares
- referrals generated
- top objections
- top acquisition source

---

## 12. Key Metrics

Track:

- lead to beta approval
- beta approval to app join
- join to first upload
- first upload to active use
- first upload to Reno It share
- referral rate
- trust issue rate

---

## 13. Mistakes to Avoid

- pushing scale before trust
- onboarding low-fit users too early
- treating every sign-up as success
- hiding OCR uncertainty
- confusing beta invite with Sanctuary invite
- overselling premium before core value is felt

---

## 14. First 50 User Goal

The goal is not "50 accounts."

The goal is:

- 50 real users with real family medication context
- enough first-upload behavior to understand trust
- enough Sanctuary behavior to validate coordination value
- enough Reno It behavior to test growth loop

If those 50 users trust the wedge, growth becomes much easier.
