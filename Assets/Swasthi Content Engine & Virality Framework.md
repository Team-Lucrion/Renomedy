# Renomedy Content Engine & Virality Framework

## Phase 3: The Virality Framework

Every piece of content produced for Renomedy follows a 6-point psychological framework designed for the Indian family dynamic.

| Component | Description | Example for Renomedy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook** | A high-tension opening that stops the scroll. | "Stop taking your BP medicine with tea!" |
| **Pattern Interrupt** | A visual or auditory surprise. | A close-up of a prescription with "illegible" handwriting being "decoded." |
| **Emotional Trigger** | Tapping into family care or fear of error. | "You're not just giving a pill; you're giving a future." |
| **Education** | The "Aha!" moment of clarity. | Explaining the difference between "Before Food" and "Empty Stomach." |
| **CTA** | A clear, low-friction next step. | "Save this for your next doctor visit." |
| **Share Trigger** | Why would someone send this to a group? | "Forward this to your family WhatsApp group to save a life." |
