# Renomedy Research Findings: Indian Medication Literacy & Caregiver Pain Points

## 1. The Literacy Gap
- **Understanding Deficit**: Studies show ~37% of patients in Indian OPDs have a poor understanding of their prescriptions.
- **Complexity**: Higher number of medications correlates with lower understanding.
- **Labels**: Many patients are unaware that product labels exist or how to read them.

## 2. Common Errors in Indian Households
- **Timing Errors**: 45.1% of caregiver mistakes are giving medicines at the wrong time.
- **Frequency Errors**: 21.6% involve incorrect frequency of doses.
- **Refusal Management**: Caregivers struggle with medication refusal, especially in chronic conditions (e.g., mental health).
- **Hospital-to-Home Gap**: 1 in 3 patients are at risk of medication errors in hospitals, which often carry over to home care due to lack of discharge counseling.

## 3. Caregiver Challenges
- **Multiple Dependents**: Indian families often have a "sandwich generation" caring for both elderly parents and children.
- **Language Barriers**: Prescriptions are usually in English, while the caregiver's primary language may be different.
- **Handwriting**: Illegible doctor handwriting remains a massive barrier to "clarity."

## 4. Market Trends
- **Trust & Authority**: Healthcare content in India is shifting towards "Doctor-led" or "Authority-led" trust building.
- **Short-form Video**: High engagement for medical awareness and "how-to" videos on Instagram/YouTube.
- **WhatsApp**: The primary channel for family communication and "medical advice" (often misinformation), presenting a massive opportunity for a "Clarity" brand to intervene.

## 5. Strategic Opportunity for Renomedy
- **Positioning**: Move from just a "tracker" to a "clarity" platform.
- **Hook**: "Decoding the Doctor's Hand" or "Prescription to Peace of Mind."
- **Shareability**: Focus on content that can be shared in "Family WhatsApp Groups" to correct common myths.
